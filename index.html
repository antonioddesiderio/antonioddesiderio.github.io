Welcome

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the ISI Foundation, working within the Complex Connections Lab (Northeastern University London).

With a background in physics, I study the digital and physical traces we leave behind to quantitatively understand human dynamics. My research draws on diverse datasets and methods from the Physics of Complex Systems, within the broader framework of Computational Social Science.

In the digital sphere, I investigate how online coordination can trigger financial events and shape news consumption. In the physical world, I explore how urban design influences human mobility and food networks.

If you have a question or find something interesting here, feel free to get in touch.
I usually reply quickly.
