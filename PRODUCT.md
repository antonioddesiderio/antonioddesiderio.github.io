# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, fellow researchers, and students. They are looking for professional information, research publications, and a sense of Antonio's technical expertise and personality.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio and academic hub that showcases research work, educational background, and side projects (like games and manga interests) in a premium, interactive way.

## Brand Personality
- **Clean**: Minimalist layout with focus on content.
- **Premium**: High-quality interactions and polished visual details.
- **Technical**: Subtle nods to code, research, and interactive tech (Three.js).

Emotional goals: Confidence, Curiosity, Professionalism.

## Anti-references
- **Notion**: Avoid the blocky, document-first, "digital garden" aesthetic.
- **WordPress**: Avoid the generic, template-heavy, blog-first feel.

## Design Principles
- **Interactive Professionalism**: Use motion and interactivity (Three.js, particles) to show skill, but never at the expense of content readability.
- **Content Hierarchy**: Research and experience must be easily findable; games and manga are "extra" delight.
- **Precision Craft**: Every transition and layout choice should feel intentional and fine-tuned, reflecting an academic's attention to detail.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA target.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for the intro and background animations.
- High contrast for academic text (publications, CV).
